About the model

This app runs the image classifier from SCIN_skin_condition_classification_baseline.ipynb. It looks at a single photo and estimates which of {{ card.num_classes }} skin conditions it most resembles. It does not use symptoms, body location, duration or any other information a doctor would ask about.

Limitations

  • Compare the accuracy with two simple baselines: always answering "{{ card.majority_label }}" would score {{ card.majority_share }} on this test set, and random guessing about {{ card.chance }}.{% if card.never_correct %} {{ card.never_correct }} of the {{ card.num_classes }} conditions were never correctly identified in testing.{% endif %}
  • Each case is reduced to its single most likely condition, while dermatologists often gave a list of possibilities.
  • Rare conditions, and anything outside these {{ card.num_classes }}, are not covered. The model will still return one of these names for any photo.
  • SCIN is crowdsourced from people in the United States. Results may differ for other populations, skin tones and cameras.
