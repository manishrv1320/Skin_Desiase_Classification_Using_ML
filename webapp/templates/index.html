Taking a usable photo
  • Use daylight or bright, even light. Avoid flash glare.
  • Fill most of the frame with the affected area, with a little normal skin around it.
  • Hold the camera steady about 15–30 cm from the skin so the texture is in focus.
  • One area per photo. Crop out clothing, jewellery and background where you can.
